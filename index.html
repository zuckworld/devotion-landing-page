Everything you need

A quiet place for daily Scripture

Six core experiences, designed to work together without overwhelming you.

Complete Bible

Offline KJV bundled with the app. Switch translations when connected. Paper, sepia, and night reading themes with adjustable font sizes.

Daily Devotionals

Fresh devotionals every day with scripture, confession, and prayer. Read or listen offline. Search by title, verse, or theme.

Hymn Library

Browse public-domain hymns with lyrics, composer stories, and background. Search by title, lyrics, or author.

Unified Search

One search for Bible verses, devotionals, hymns, notes, and audio history. Results that open exactly where you need to go.

Listen Anywhere

Device-native text-to-speech for Bible and devotionals. Playback speed controls, mini-player, and downloadable audio.

Notes & Prayer

Verse highlights, bookmarks, Bible notes with backend sync, and a personal prayer journal that stays private.

Immersive Reading

Read the Word the way that suits your eyes.

Three carefully tuned themes, smooth verse-by-verse layout, and reading history that remembers exactly where you left off.

  • Offline KJV, no connection required
  • Bookmarks, highlights, and personal notes
  • Text-to-speech with natural voice
  • Continue reading from your Home screen
  • Download passages for deep study
